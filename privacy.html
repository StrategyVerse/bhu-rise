Privacy Policy

This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Who we are

Bhū Rise Ventures (“Bhū Rise”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is a pan-India land investment advisory. This policy applies to bhurise.com and to our official social media pages, and to the personal information you share with us through them. For anything in this policy, you can reach us at info@bhurise.com.

Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us. Specifically, when you submit our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name;
  • Your email address;
  • Your phone number;
  • The area of interest you select; and
  • The message you write to us, including any details you choose to share about your investment goal, budget, location or timeline.

If you contact or interact with us through our official social media pages — for example by sending a direct message, comment or enquiry — we also receive the information you choose to share there, such as your name or social media handle, your message, and any contact or investment details you include.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this website, and we do not build profiles of your browsing. Like most websites, our hosting provider may process standard technical information (such as your IP address) to serve pages securely.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation;
  • provide and follow up on the advisory services you ask about; and
  • keep a record of our correspondence with you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else’s marketing.

Legal basis and consent

When you submit the contact form, you consent to us using your details to respond to and act on your enquiry, as described above. You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to info@bhurise.com — though doing so may mean we can no longer respond to an open enquiry.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted third parties to run this website. They process limited data only to provide their service to us:

  • Web3Forms — delivers your contact-form submission to our inbox.
  • Google Maps — embedded on our contact page to show our office location; loading it may share your IP address and device information with Google.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used across the site.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts and serves this website.
  • Social media platforms — we maintain official pages on third-party platforms (such as LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook). When you interact with us there, your information is also handled under that platform’s own privacy policy, and anything you post publicly may be visible to others.

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside India. Where that happens, we rely on their own safeguards for such transfers.

Cookies

Bhū Rise does not set its own advertising or analytics cookies. The embedded Google Map on our contact page may set cookies when it loads. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and correspondence data only for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain a reasonable business record, after which we delete or anonymise it. If you would like us to delete your details sooner, just ask.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your personal information; and
  • withdraw a consent you previously gave.

To make a request, email us at info@bhurise.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security — please avoid sending sensitive financial or identity documents through the contact form.

Children

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
